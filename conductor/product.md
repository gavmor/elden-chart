# Product Guide

## Overview
Elden Ring & Deadlock Visualizer is a high-fidelity interactive SPA that allows players to analyze in-game armor and item stats across multiple dimensions using a scatter plot visualization.

## Key Features
- **Multi-Game Selection:** Toggle between Elden Ring armor data and Deadlock items directly from the UI header.
- **Scatter Plot Visualization:** Plots armor pieces or Deadlock items based on multiple selectable, dynamically generated axes (e.g., Weight, Poise, physical properties, or cost). Includes Calculated Metrics such as Effective Health (eHP) and Total Integrated Armor for advanced trade-off analysis.
- **Custom Build Set Planner:** Allows players to select and compare a custom set of armor pieces.
- **Dynamic Heatmaps & Coloring:** Colors datapoints by categorical grouping or continuous numerical stats.
- **Dynamic Engine Baselines:** Automatically calculates baseline exchange rates for Weapon Damage, Vitality Health, and Spirit Power using multivariate linear regression.
- **Visual Outlier Clamping & Active Items:** Extreme outliers are visually clamped while retaining precise tooltips, and items with active abilities feature distinct graphical indicators.
- **Side-by-Side Attribute Comparison Modal:** Detailed tabular comparison between different selected items.
- **Responsive & Premium Aesthetic:** Utilizes floating transparent item previews, halos, and a dark-fantasy theme.

## Target Audience
- Elden Ring players and theorycrafters looking to explore trade-offs of different armor attributes.
- Deadlock players looking to visualize item properties and cost-benefit ratios.

## Design Philosophy
Provide an immersive, fast, and highly visual experience directly referencing the aesthetics of the game itself, with clean UI/UX paradigms to handle complex data analysis.
